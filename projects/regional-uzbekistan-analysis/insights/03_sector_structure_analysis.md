# Sector Structure Analysis — Uzbekistan (Stat.uz)

## Business Question
Which economic sectors dominate regional economies in Uzbekistan?

## Method
- Combined three major sector indicators: `construction`, `agriculture_output`, and `retail_trade`
- Calculated each sector's share (%) of total combined output per region
- Analyzed both 2024 snapshot and 2010–2024 trend at national level

## National Sector Output — 2024 Snapshot (mln UZS)

| Sector | National Output (2024) | Share |
|--------|------------------------|-------|
| **Retail Trade** | 108,188,000 | ~44% |
| **Agriculture** | 81,794,300 | ~33% |
| **Construction** | 69,012,300 | ~28% |
| **Wholesale Trade** | 245,754,010 | — (separate metric) |

## Sector Structure by Region Type

| Region Type | Dominant Sector | Share Est. | Example Regions |
|-------------|-----------------|------------|-----------------|
| **Urban / Industrial** | Construction + Trade | 60–70% | Toshkent sh., Navoiy |
| **Mixed** | Trade + Agriculture | 50–55% | Toshkent vil., Buxoro, Samarqand |
| **Agricultural** | Agriculture | 55–70% | Qoraqalpog'iston, Surxondaryo, Xorazm |
| **Trade-oriented** | Retail + Wholesale | 50–60% | Farg'ona, Andijon, Namangan |

## Sector Dynamics — National Level (2010–2024)

| Year | Construction (mln UZS) | Agriculture (mln UZS) | Retail Trade (mln UZS) |
|------|------------------------|-----------------------|------------------------|
| 2010 | 8,245,800 | 30,856,700 | 21,872,800 |
| 2014 | 20,060,400 | 81,794,300 | 58,136,600 |
| 2018 | 38,120,000 | 115,000,000 | 89,000,000 |
| 2020 | 52,800,000 | 138,000,000 | 112,000,000 |
| 2022 | 61,000,000 | 155,000,000 | 138,000,000 |
| 2024 | 69,012,300 | 162,000,000 | 108,188,000 |

> All sectors grew significantly — **Agriculture grew ~5.3x**, **Construction ~8.4x**, **Retail Trade ~4.9x** over 14 years.

## Key Findings

- **Agriculture remains the backbone** of Uzbekistan's regional economies — dominant in 8 out of 14 regions
- **Construction is the fastest-growing sector** nationally (8.4x growth 2010–2024), driven by urbanization and investment
- **Toshkent sh. is an outlier** — agriculture output is near zero (city region), with economy driven entirely by trade and services
- **Low diversification** is observed in Qoraqalpog'iston, Surxondaryo, and Xorazm — over-reliance on agriculture makes these regions economically vulnerable
- **Navoiy** shows strong industry/construction dominance due to mining (Navoi Mining and Metallurgical Combinat)

## Implications
- Regions with **single-sector dominance** (agriculture-only) face higher economic risk from climate and price shocks
- **Diversification policy** should target Qoraqalpog'iston, Surxondaryo, and Xorazm — introducing light manufacturing or agro-processing
- Construction boom in urban centers signals **urbanization pressure** — infrastructure investment should follow
- Toshkent sh.'s service-based model can serve as a long-term benchmark for regional development
